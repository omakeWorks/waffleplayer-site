Waffle Player Roadmap

This page lists what we are considering for future versions of Waffle Player. Everything here is under consideration, with no set schedule, and is not a commitment. Please base your purchase decision on the current features and supported formats listed on the Microsoft Store page. You can use the 30-day free trial to check whether your own footage plays.

Editing formats (under consideration)

We are considering support for these formats, which are widely used in video production:

  • ProRes (422, 422 HQ, 4444, and others)
  • DNxHD / DNxHR
  • MXF

Other formats (under consideration)

  • AVI, MTS / M2TS, WMV, FLV

When support is added, we will announce it in the Microsoft Store release notes and on this page.

Send requests to omake.works@gmail.com.
