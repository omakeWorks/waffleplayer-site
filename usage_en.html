How to use Sand Player

Sand Player home / 日本語

Sand Player puts two videos or images side by side, with a difference view and a blend (overlay) view in between, so you can find what changed. The red boxes and numbers in the images match the numbers in the text.

1. Load videos

  • Drop onto the left half (L) of the window to load into L, or the right half (R) to load into R
  • Drop as many as you like; they are added to that side's playlist in order. Drop a folder to load its subfolders too
  • Files opened with Sand Player from File Explorer (MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM) go into L
  • Playback starts automatically once both L and R have a video

Supported formats

TypeFormats
VideoMP4, MOV, MKV, WebM (H.264, H.265/HEVC, VP8, VP9, AV1)
ImagesPNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, BMP, AVIF

Editing codecs such as ProRes and DNxHD, AVI, MTS, WMV, FLV, MXF, and TIFF images are not supported. A video that can't be played shows "This video can't be played" in its frame. Playing H.265 requires a GPU with hardware H.265 decoding.

2. Three views

Difference

Shows the difference between L and R. Matching areas are black and only the differences light up, so you can spot any difference right away.

Blend

Overlays R on L, semi-transparent (1). Change R's opacity with the slider at the bottom of the middle view (2) — it starts at 50%.

Both

Switch views with "Display mode" in the gear menu.

  • Even during playback, frames with the same timestamp are compared. Put the same video on both sides and the difference stays black
  • When lengths differ, the shorter video holds on its last frame while the comparison continues

Step one frame with ← / → or the mouse wheel, 1/3 second with Shift, and 1 second with Alt.

When L and R have different frame rates, a warning appears at the top of the window. In that case, the sync between them is slightly off.

4. Use the playlists

  • Open: move the mouse to the left or right edge of the window; it closes when you move away. Press L or the Playlist button to keep it open
  • Switch: W / S for the previous / next video in L, ↑ / ↓ for R, and PageUp / PageDown for both. Clicking an item also switches. Both sides restart from the beginning when you switch
  • Reorder: drag items. Drag to the other playlist to move them between L and R
  • Insert: drop files between playlist items to add them there
  • Select several (1): Ctrl + click to add items one by one, Shift + click for a range. Drag selected items together. Press Esc to clear the selection
  • Remove (2): drop items on "Drag here to remove from list" at the bottom, or select them and press Delete

5. Show captions

Press C or the CC button to cycle through:

ModeShows
CC (off)Nothing
CC₁ (detail)File name, resolution, frame rate, codec, duration, total frames, current time and frame number
CC₂ (name only)The file name, large in the middle
  • Move captions to any corner by dragging them, or with Ctrl + arrow keys
  • Change their size with "Caption size" in the gear menu

7. HDR video

For comparison, HDR (HLG / PQ) video is shown as its signal values without tone mapping, not with its intended HDR look. This way L, R, difference, and blend all show the same colors. Portrait HDR video from iPhone is supported.

8. Add notes in capture mode

Press the camera button to turn the current view, with L, R, and the comparison, into a still image you can write on. Use it to send the differences you found, or revision notes, as an image right away.
